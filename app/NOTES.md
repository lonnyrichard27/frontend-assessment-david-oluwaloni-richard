## I was allowed one table/list library and used none. @tanstack/react-virtual was the obvious choice for this but is ruled out by an issue which is that virtualisation removes rows from the DOM, which breaks Ctrl-P and find-in-page. @tanstack/react-table would have been nice to have, but its column definitions would have cost more lines than the filtering logic it replaced. Plain .filter() over a <table> was smaller and kept every row printable.
